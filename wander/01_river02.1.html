Yes, you want to see what lies beneath the surface. The water is refreshingly cool against your fur as you dunk your head in the river. You are met with a flurry of colors. All sorts of fish speed past you, their scales catching the sunlight for a moment before they are gone with the current. Reds and yellows and purples mix, creating a dazzling rainbow under the water. You exhale in surprise as a fish stops to examine you. Bubbles flood your vision. You are as alien to the fish as it is to you. But is this not a beautiful moment of worlds colliding? How lucky you are to witness such overflowing life. The fish forgives your intrusion, gently gliding away. You come up for air, gasping as the sun beats down on you. For a moment, your eyes burn with water and light, the elements colliding with your physical being. You are all connected, in one way or another. The thought lingers as you shake the water from you and look around. There are mountains in the distance, their high peaks reaching for the sky. You could return to the fork in the road and explore the other path. Or you could venture deeper into the forest.
