You decide to follow the sunlight, slowly making your way out of the forest. You step out from beneath the trees' shaded cover, and for a moment, the sun is all you can see. Warmth beats down on your retinas, but when your eyes adjust, you gasp. You've entered a beautiful flower field. The grass sprawls for as far as the eye can see, gentle hills rolling towards the horizon. And the flowers- oh, the flowers! There are so many of them, forming a vibrant rainbow that sways with every breath of the wind. A pleasant fragrance fills your nose, and you eagerly trot into the tall grass, sniffing at the wildflowers. Some have a subtle and refreshing scent, while others are sickly sweet and honeyed. You pause when you hear faint buzzing. You settle on the ground, tucking your paws beneath yourself, and wait. Your patience pays off, and soon enough, a round and fluffy bumblebee enters your sights. You watch as it flits from flower to flower, sometimes settling within the petals for a moment of respite. If you could tuck away in a flower's shell, you would, too. The bumblebee buzzes onward, and you decide to follow it. As you venture deeper into the field, you lose sight of it, but something else catches your eye. A small flower sprouting from the ground, barely visible from above. Though it rests in the shade, its golden petals shimmer as if they emanate light themselves. The flower rustles in the wind, its glow increasing for a moment before it dims again. Will you pick the flower or leave it?
