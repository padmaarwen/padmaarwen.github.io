You decide to pick the flower. Its petals are so enchanting, and if you press it properly, its beauty will be preserved for ages. It will be a nice memento to show Mama and Papa—a reminder of your time here, that you and your adventures were real. You tuck the flower behind your ear and continue onwards. You begin to hear a faint buzzing. When it goes on and on and on, you swivel your head this way and that. Ah, it seems you've gained a friend. The little bumblebee from before bumps your nose gently before flying up and settling in the flower. It accompanies you until the edge of the flower field, wherein it returns to its companions. You venture onwards. There are mountains in the distance, their high peaks reaching for the sky. You could return to the fork in the road and explore the other path. Or you could venture deeper into the forest.
