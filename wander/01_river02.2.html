Let it be; some things are better left alone. Instead, your attention is drawn to the way the sun dances over the water. Glimmers and sparkles dance across the cerulean surface, as if the river itself was liquid gold. You wonder what it would be like to dance as they do. Deciding to try, you leap across the grassy banks, twirling to the beat of the rushing water. It is exhilarating, to move as if you were a petal on the wind. You collapse by the river, heaving in air. The soft grass tickles you, fluttering in the breeze. You watch the clouds pass by, studying their white fluff. When the last sheep-shaped cloud leaves your vision and only the blue expanse remains, you decide it is time to move on. You look around. There are mountains in the distance, their high peaks reaching for the sky. You could return to the fork in the road and explore the other path. Or you could venture deeper into the forest.
