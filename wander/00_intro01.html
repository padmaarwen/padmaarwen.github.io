The sunbeam slowly travels away from your spot on the windowsill, disturbing your slumber. You crack one eye open, grumbling as the warmth leaves you. You scoot over so that you are back under the sunlight. The sunbeam moves. You scoot again. After the fourth time, you scoot right into the frame of the window. The sunbeam has escaped you permanently and taken your nap with it. You paw at your eyes, rubbing the sleep from them. You stretch out, yawning slightly. You have little to do and the whole day ahead of you. Perhaps it's time to get some fresh air. Jump from the windowsill.
