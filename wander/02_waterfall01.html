The forest beckons you further. Everything grows more vibrant and lively. Critters rustle in the leaves, both below your feet and above your head. You can feel the trees grow older, the roots wiser. Soon, you begin to hear the sound of water. You wonder if the forest has led you back to the river, but this feels... different. As if you are entering the heart of the forest. The sound grows louder and louder, until you part a curtain of vines and step out into a sunny clearing. The flora here grows stronger and stranger as it approaches the water. You realize you've entered the heart of the forest when you see the waterfall at the other end of the clearing, cutting sharp and clear through rocky cliffs. It pours into a spring, brimming with life. You watch as dragonflies flit around lily pads, settling on flowers for a moment before taking off again. The buzzing of insects is a soft but constant accompaniment to the loud sounds of falling water. You approach the edge of the pond, peering down at the nearest lily pad. A light green frog hops around on the surface, much to the chagrin of the brown toad trying to rest. Though they did not speak, you know the two amphibians called your name. Will you follow the jumpy frog or the sleepy toad?
