You decide to explore the mountains. As you climb through the foothills, the air gets colder and the flora around you begins to change. You notice more and more jagged stones puncturing the treeline, and soon you are walking over spongy moss rather than soft grass. The leafy trees of the valley below give way to sturdy pines that pack densely together. Everything here is quieter, as if it rests in a deep slumber. You tread quietly, lest you wake the earth. Looking up, you get a true sense of the mountain's height. Even the clouds seem to have parted for the giant, swirling around it in gentle circles. When you clear the forest, you find yourself at the true base of the mountain. A large, rocky opening beckons you deeper into the earth, a faint glow coming from within. Yet your eye travels to the side, where tiered stone formations lead up and away. Will you descend to the depths or ascend to the very zenith?
