You land on your feet, as always. You pitter-patter away from the house, down the well-worn path. You never know where the road will take you. Each time, it is a pleasant surprise. Slowly, the road enters a forest of tall oak trees. Their thick and sturdy branches spread out towards the sky, forming a lush green canopy. Sunlight filters in through the leaves, illuminating your way with dappled patterns. You trot along the path, admiring the vibrant flora and fauna around you. You slow to a stop when you reach a fork in the road. To one direction, the path continues into the forest, and you can hear the sound of rushing water. To the other, you can see the path curve outwards into the sunlight. Will you go towards the water or the sunlight?
