Your path to the top of the mountain is long and winding, yet it seems that at every turn, the rocks are perfectly placed for you to continue. You wonder if the soft sound of shifting earth is a trick of the mind. Still, your journey is nothing but serene. As you ascend, the pines become sparser and the air thinner. Soon, even the moss gives way to the snowy grey wilderness. Strangely, you don't feel the cold. You take a final step up and walk onto a flat outlook. You are so high that the clouds are within reaching distance. Below, the valley and the forest sprawl out, continuing into the horizon. In this moment, you wish you had wings. To jump and feel the wind lift you up, to see this sight every day as you soar through the sky... it terrifies you and excites you. Perhaps in the next life. You turn, eyes landing on a rough boulder that hadn't been there before. You come up to it. There is a small lantern tucked into one of its crevices. An offering, perhaps. Whether in remembrance or in reverence, you do not know. You light it. Its warmth glows in a stark contrast to the snow whirling in the air. The ground beneath you seems to move, emitting a soft rumble. You bow to the mountain, knowing you have fulfilled its wish. You realize the day is running short when the first streaks of orange and pink begin to paint the sky. Your adventure is almost at an end. You know where to go.
